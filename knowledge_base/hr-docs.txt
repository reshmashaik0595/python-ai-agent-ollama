# HR Documents

## Overview
This file contains a summary of key HR resources and documents used by employees and managers.

## Core HR Policies
- Hiring process
- Leave policy
- Medical and wellness policy
- Travel and expense policy
- Code of conduct
- Performance management guidelines

## Employee Support
HR provides support for:
- onboarding and offboarding
- compensation and payroll questions
- leave and attendance tracking
- health benefits and claims
- grievance handling and policy clarification

## Required Employee Documentation
- Government-issued ID
- Bank account details for payroll
- Emergency contact information
- Educational and previous employment records if required
- Tax and statutory forms

## Manager Resources
Managers are expected to:
- maintain accurate records for their teams
- approve leave according to policy
- ensure compliance with workplace policies
- document performance conversations and outcomes
- escalate employee concerns to HR when needed

## Confidentiality
Employee records and HR information are confidential and must be shared only with authorized personnel for legitimate business purposes.

## Useful Contacts
- HR Manager
- Payroll Team
- Benefits Administrator
- Employee Relations Lead
