# Leave Policy

## Purpose
This leave policy explains the benefits available to employees and the process for requesting time off.

## Types of Leave
### Annual Leave
- Employees earn annual leave based on tenure and employment type.
- Unused annual leave may be carried forward subject to departmental limits.
- Leave should be planned in advance and approved by the manager.

### Sick Leave
- Sick leave is provided for personal illness or medical treatment.
- Employees should inform their manager as early as possible.
- Medical certificates may be required for extended absences.

### Casual Leave
- Casual leave is intended for short, urgent personal matters.
- Requests should normally be submitted before the start of the workday.

### Maternity / Paternity Leave
- Eligible employees may take statutory leave as per applicable law.
- Leave approvals must be supported by required documents and timelines.

### Bereavement Leave
- Short-term leave may be granted in the event of a close family member's passing.
- Documentation may be requested in some cases.

## Request and Approval Process
1. Employee submits a leave request through the HR or internal workflow system.
2. Manager reviews workload and approves or rejects the request.
3. HR records the leave status and updates payroll where required.
4. Approved leave is reflected in attendance records.

## Attendance and Compliance
- Leave must be taken in line with applicable labor regulations.
- Employees are expected to maintain accurate attendance records.
- Unapproved absences may be treated according to disciplinary policy.

## Important Notes
- Leave balances may vary depending on policy year and employment terms.
- Employees should check their current leave balance before planning travel or extended breaks.
