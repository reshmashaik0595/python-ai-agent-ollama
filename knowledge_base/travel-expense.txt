# Travel Expense Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines how employees can request reimbursement for business travel and related business expenses.

## Eligibility
Employees may claim travel expenses when:
- travel is required for business duties
- the trip has been authorized by the manager or department head
- receipts and supporting documents are provided

## Approved Expense Types
The following are typically eligible for reimbursement:
- airfare or train tickets
- local transportation
- hotel accommodation
- meals during approved business travel
- parking and toll charges
- conference or event registration fees

## Before Travel
- Travel requests should be approved before booking.
- Preferred vendors or budget limits must be followed.
- Employees should check whether a company card or advance is available.

## During Travel
- Employees should keep all original receipts.
- Business-related expenses should be itemized clearly.
- Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

## Expense Reporting
Employees must submit claims with:
- purpose of travel
- travel dates
- receipts and invoices
- approved travel request reference
- account details for reimbursement

## Reimbursement Timelines
- Claims should be submitted within a defined period after the trip ends.
- Incomplete or unsupported claims may be delayed or rejected.
- Reimbursements are processed after verification by the finance team.

## Compliance and Ethics
- Claims must be truthful and accurate.
- Misrepresentation or duplicate reimbursement is grounds for review.
- The company may request supporting evidence for unusual or high-value expenses.
