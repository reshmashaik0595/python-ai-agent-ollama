# Medical Policy

## Purpose
The medical policy supports employee health, wellbeing, and access to appropriate care while maintaining fair and transparent operational practices.

## Employee Health Coverage
- Eligible employees receive medical insurance coverage as per company plan rules.
- Coverage details and enrollment windows are provided during onboarding and annual benefit updates.
- Dependents may be covered where applicable under the policy terms.

## Clinic and Treatment Support
- Employees may use network clinics and hospitals for consultations, treatment, and emergency care.
- Claims must be submitted with complete documentation as required by the provider.
- The company may reimburse eligible medical expenses according to policy limits.

## Sick Leave and Health Absences
- Employees should report illness promptly and follow attendance procedure.
- Extended absences may require medical certificates or physician recommendations.
- Managers should keep health-related matters confidential and respectful.

## Wellness and Safety
- The company encourages preventive health checkups when available.
- Employees are expected to follow workplace health and safety guidelines.
- Health emergencies should be reported immediately to the relevant supervisor or emergency contact.

## Confidentiality
Medical information is treated as confidential and shared only with authorized HR or benefits personnel when needed for policy administration.

## Claims and Documentation
Employees must submit:
- treatment details
- invoices or receipts
- claim forms
- relevant prescriptions or reports if requested

## Escalation
Questions about coverage, treatment reimbursement, or special medical leave should be directed to the HR or Benefits team.
