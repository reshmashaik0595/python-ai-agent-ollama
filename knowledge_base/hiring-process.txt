# Hiring Process

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard hiring process for all roles at the company. The goal is to ensure a fair, consistent, and efficient recruitment experience while attracting top talent.

## 1. Role Request and Approval
- A department manager submits a hiring request with the role title, business need, headcount, and budget.
- The HR team reviews the request for budget and hiring approval.
- Once approved, the role is opened for recruitment.

## 2. Job Posting and Candidate Sourcing
- The job description is created or updated by the hiring manager and HR.
- Internal job boards and external hiring channels are used to attract candidates.
- Referrals from employees are encouraged and tracked.

## 3. Screening and Shortlisting
- Applicants are reviewed against minimum qualifications and role requirements.
- HR screens for experience, education, and basic eligibility.
- Hiring managers shortlist candidates for interviews based on relevant skills and fit.

## 4. Interview Stages
Typical interview process:
1. Phone or video screening
2. Technical or role-based assessment
3. Manager interview
4. Final panel interview
5. Offer discussion

Each stage should have clear evaluation criteria and a standardized scorecard.

## 5. Decision and Offer
- Hiring managers share candidate feedback with HR.
- Final candidates are compared against selection criteria.
- Approved offers include salary, position, start date, and benefits summary.
- Background checks and reference verification are completed before final onboarding.

## 6. Onboarding
- HR sends the offer letter and collects required documents.
- New hires complete orientation and sign policy acknowledgments.
- Managers provide role-specific training and first-week goals.

## 7. Equal Opportunity and Fairness
- Hiring is based on skills, qualifications, and business needs.
- All candidates must be evaluated consistently and without discrimination.
- Requests for accommodation are handled respectfully and in compliance with policy.

## 8. Timeline Expectations
- Most hiring processes are completed within 2 to 6 weeks depending on role complexity.
- Delays due to candidate availability, approvals, or reference checks should be communicated clearly.

## Contact
For questions about recruiting procedures, contact the HR Operations team.
