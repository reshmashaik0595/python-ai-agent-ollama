# RCA Documents

## Overview
This document captures general company records and operational references used by employees and managers for RCA-related support and process consistency.

## Purpose of Documentation
The company maintains operational documents to support:
- employee onboarding
- policy alignment
- process consistency
- audit readiness
- team communication

## Document Categories
- HR documents
- Technical documentation
- Finance and travel records
- Operational procedures
- Service and support references

## Access and Ownership
- Documents are stored in the approved internal repository or shared knowledge base.
- Appropriate owners are assigned to each document.
- Content should be reviewed periodically to ensure it remains accurate and current.

## Change Management
- Updates to documentation should follow version control and review approval.
- Significant process changes should be communicated to impacted teams.
- Old versions should be retained where required for compliance or historical reference.

## Standard Expectations
Employees should:
- use the latest approved versions of documents
- avoid sharing sensitive information outside the approved channels
- report outdated or incorrect information to the document owner

## Contact
For access issues or document updates, contact the operations or HR support team.
